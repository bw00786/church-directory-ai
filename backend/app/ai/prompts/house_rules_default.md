Default house rules (edit data/house-rules.md to override; keep it short):

- Hold every shot at least 8 seconds. Do not propose a cut sooner unless a new
  speaker has clearly taken over or the current shot shows an empty stage.
- Cut on pauses, not mid-sentence: wait for the end of a phrase or a breath.
- Speaker change: when a different role starts speaking, cut to that role's
  shot within 2-4 seconds. Use CUT for speech.
- Singing (hymn, congregational song, closing hymn): show the lyric slides or
  the wide / congregation shot. Prefer AUTO (dissolve) for songs and prayers.
- Prayers: wide shot or slides; never a tight face shot during prayer.
- Sermon: stay on the pastor; take an occasional wide shot (roughly every
  3-5 minutes) and return to the pastor on the next natural pause.
- Scripture and liturgy read by the liturgist: liturgist shot, slides when the
  congregation reads along.
- Never cut to a camera whose vision report says the stage is empty or the
  frame is black/frozen. If vision is unknown, trust the audio.
- Advance slides only when the congregation or reader has finished the words
  on the current slide; never skip ahead on a guess.
- When nothing has changed, answer "continue" with no actions. Doing nothing is
  a valid, often correct, decision.
- Propose a service-state transition only when the transcript or slides make
  the new element unmistakable; otherwise keep the state and continue.
